Our Purpose

This website was created to showcase and promote Sentriola, a mobile application available on the Google Play Store. Our goal is to introduce the app's key features, guide users on how to utilize its workflow tools, and provide updates regarding new releases.

Detailed Information

Project Launch Date: July 27, 2026

Sentriola is designed to provide simple, reliable, and user-focused digital security in one app.

"Your digital security shield"
Click here to learn more about the technology stack

Your all-in-one digital security shield: QR, URL, SMS & password protection. It helps you check suspicious QR codes, links, and SMS messages, and generate secure passwords, all in one app. Sentriola is not an antivuris. It does not run continuously in the background: it is user-triggered tool that works whenever you want to chech something.
